Where your data lives

  • Only in this browser, on this device. Everything is saved in the browser's localStorage — a small storage area the browser keeps on your disk for this page.
  • Nothing is sent anywhere. The page has no server, no account and no analytics, and it carries a security policy that makes the browser block any attempt to contact the internet.
  • Who can see it: anyone who can use this browser profile on this computer, and browser extensions you have given permission to read pages. It is not encrypted — use a device login/password.
  • It can be lost: clearing browsing data / site data, using a private window, or switching browser or computer means you won't see it. Download a backup now and then.
